# CONTEXT.md Format

```md
# {Context Name}

{One or two sentence description of the context.}

## Language

**Order**:
{One or two sentence definition.}
_Avoid_: Purchase, transaction
```

- Pick one canonical term and list alternatives under `_Avoid_`.
- Keep definitions to one or two sentences describing what a term is.
- Include only terms specific to this project's domain.
- Group terms under subheadings when natural clusters emerge.
